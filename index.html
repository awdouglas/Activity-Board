Welcome to the Idea Board!

Your initials

When you first visit, you're asked for your initials. They identify what you've posted, commented, or voted on. You can change them anytime with the "change" link next to your badge at the top of the page.

💡 Add an idea

The "Add an idea" card at the top is collapsible — click its header (or the ▾ arrow) to expand or collapse it. Fill in your idea's name, optional description, website, day/time, and tags, then hit Submit idea.

📋 The Board tab

  • ▲ Upvote — click the arrow on the left of any idea to vote for it. Click again to remove your vote.
  • ⭐ Star — a private bookmark only you can see, to keep track of your personal favorites. This is only stored on your browser.
  • ✎ Edit / 🗑 Delete — only appear on ideas you posted.
  • 🕒 Scheduling suggestions — anyone can propose a day/time for an idea and others can vote on it. The most-upvoted suggestion "wins," unless someone marks 🔒 Reservation Made — that always takes priority over votes.
  • 💬 Comments — add comments, upvote them, sort by newest/oldest/most-upvoted, and edit or delete your own.
  • Use ⊕ Expand all / ⊖ Collapse all to open or close every idea's comments and suggestions at once.

⚙ Filters & sorting

Click ⚙ Filters to narrow the list by tag, who suggested it, a specific day, ideas with reservations, or your starred ideas. Use the sort dropdown to order ideas by newest, oldest, most upvoted, recently updated, or most engagement (comment count).

🗓️ The Calendar tab

See everything with a day/time on an actual calendar. Switch between Week / 3 Days / 1 Day views and navigate with ← Prev / Next →. Each idea's time is auto-resolved from its own posted time and any scheduling suggestions.

  • Events are color-coded by vote count — a heat map from pale yellow (fewest votes) to saturated orange (most votes), ordered left-to-right.
  • 🔒 Reservation Made events are always shown in red and always placed first — they can't be out-voted.
  • A diagonal stripe means the end time wasn't specified and was defaulted to 1 hour — treat it as unverified.
  • Click any event to jump back to the Board and highlight that idea.

🔄 Staying in sync

The board updates live as others make changes. If something looks out of date, click ↻ refresh next to the sync status at the top.

☀️ Light / 🌙 Dark mode

Use the toggle switch in the header to flip between dark and light themes — your choice is remembered on this device.
